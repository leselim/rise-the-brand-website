RISE THE BRAND — WEBSITE UPDATE

All changes are in rise-the-brand/source (the folder that deploys to risethebrand.co.za).

1. Trade prices are off the public site
   - src/pages/Trade.jsx: the reseller and distributor price tables and the tab
     switch are gone. The page now explains the two tiers in words and keeps the
     application form. Applicants are emailed the current rate sheet.
   - src/data/store.js: "tradeRates" replaced by "tradeTiers" (plain descriptions)
     and "tradeNote". Prices now live only in the PDFs in /rate-sheets.
   - src/styles/pages.css: small "tier" styles added for the new section.
   - Help page FAQ, footer, header, home page and contact text no longer point
     at "trade rates"; they point at the Trade page and the application.

2. Retail prices on the shop
   Updated in src/data/store.js to the new prices:
     Multi Purpose Cleaner  750 ml   R100
     Dishwashing Liquid     750 ml   R35
     Pine Gel               1 L      R40
     Footwear Cleaner       200 ml   R100

3. "Or 4 easy payments of ..." removed
   settings.installments is now 0, since no instalment provider is connected.
   Set it back to 4 when one is.

4. Pricing rule
   Retail (the website price) is the anchor. Resellers pay 75% of retail,
   distributors 60%, same product and size on every list. Trade prices are
   never shown on the site.

5. Rate sheets and explainer
   /rate-sheets/Rise_Distributor_Rates.pdf and Rise_Reseller_Rates.pdf are the
   current price lists to email to approved applicants.
   /rate-sheets/Rise_Pricing_Why_It_Changed.pdf explains the new structure.

TO PUBLISH
   cd rise-the-brand/source
   npm install
   npm run build        (creates source/dist)
   Push to main and the workflow deploys it, or upload dist to your host.
